The challenges don't come with a manual.

Projects change direction. Teams disagree. Communities have competing needs. People working in project and community management navigate difficult decisions every day—often without a straightforward answer.

Diaries of Project Managers moves beyond the traditional conference format. Rather than another set of lectures, it brings experienced practitioners into a room to examine real situations, challenge assumptions and learn from how others solve problems.

“You don't have to have the perfect answer to ask a better question.”
What Diaries of Project Managers is built around

What happens in the room.

The day follows a simple progression: bring your experience, unpack the problem, explore different approaches and decide what you will take back to work.

  1. 01

    The Room

    An informal, intimate space where people can discuss real challenges openly, without the pressure of being evaluated.

  2. 02

    The Problems

    Participants share project and community challenges anonymously. These become the starting point for practical group exercises.

  3. 03

    The Conversations

    Small groups interrogate each situation: What is happening? What are we missing? Who is affected? What would we do first?

  4. 04

    The Reflection

    Every participant identifies ideas to apply, assumptions to reconsider and people they want to continue learning with.

Who should be in the room?

We're looking for professionals with practical experience managing people, programmes, projects, communities or partnerships—people who can contribute as much as they learn.

  • Project, product & programme managers
  • Community managers & leads
  • Operations leads
  • Ecosystem builders
  • Startup & NGO professionals

This is not a beginner career seminar. Participation is intentionally limited to 25 people.

Sessions worth showing up for.

Real challenges take the lead, supported by structured exercises, facilitator input and open peer conversations.

  • Facilitator-led conversation
  • The Problem Lab — real-world cases
  • Hands-on workshop
  • Peer discussions & reflection

What you take with you.

The value isn't in the number of talks you attend. It's in what you can approach differently afterwards.

Ideas to apply
